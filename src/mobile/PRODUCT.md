# BHOME Mobile

## Product

BHOME supports rental-property management for owners, staff and tenants. Staff Mobile is an operational workspace used on a phone while managing assigned facilities, rooms and assets. The existing Owner and Tenant surfaces are outside the November 27 Staff redesign.

## Staff needs

- Identify the active assigned facility and switch only between authorized facilities.
- Find today's work and incidents quickly, then open the correct task.
- Manage rooms and residents, record utilities, handle incidents, and complete the existing contract, inspection and handover workflows.
- Preserve the distinction between direct inspection and inherited evidence. Keep approval, version, signature and photo requirements intact.
- Never present missing API data as zero, invent an assignment, or expose a different role's actions.

## Success

Staff can reach routine actions from Home, scan lists without repetitive prose, complete forms without covered fields or lost drafts, and read truthful loading, error, empty and workflow states. The four existing tabs, Staff permissions, API contracts, evidence and navigation guards remain authoritative.

Platform: adaptive React Native / Expo (iOS, Android, web preview); Vietnamese UI.
